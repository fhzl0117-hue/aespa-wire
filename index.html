SEOUL — AUG 27

Winter turns to rock for solo single "Speed of Summer"

The aespa vocalist steps outside the group's synth-pop lane for an alternative punk-rock track made with digital media channel Dingo, released August 28. Korean press described it as capturing "a moment of freedom speeding through a summer night with the radio on full volume" — a stretch for Winter's usual clean, high vocal range.

CHICAGO — AUG 2

aespa make their Lollapalooza debut in the rain, with a Ty Dolla $ign surprise

The group's first appearance at the festival ran through heavy rain at Grant Park, with a surprise guest turn from Ty Dolla $ign on "Switchblade." In interviews around the show, Ningning and Giselle spoke about the difficulty of showing who they really are inside K-pop idol life — even as their music, they said, is "something that only aespa can do."

Desk 02 · Review

Revisiting the catalogue, track by track.

Text only, always — no reposted photos or video frames on this desk.

4.3 / 5EDITOR'S STAMP
"LEMONADE is aespa treating genre as a costume change — pop-rock one track, hip-hop the next, a doo-wop wind-up-doll hook two songs later — and somehow still sounding like one group the whole way through."

LEMONADE (2026): eleven tracks, two features, and a second full album that debuted at No. 1 on Korea's Circle Chart. The set opens with pre-release single "WDA (Whole Different Animal)," a hip-hop-leaning collaboration with G-DRAGON, before the title track needle-drops into a brighter, hookier register than the group's usual maximalist production.

Standouts include "Camouflage," built around vocal-experimentation the group has singled out as a personal favorite, and "My Plan," which folds a wind-up-doll sound effect into its bridge. The album closes with a bonus duet version of the title track featuring Becky G. It went on to chart at No. 2 on Japan's Oricon, No. 1 in Spain, and No. 15 on the Billboard 200.
